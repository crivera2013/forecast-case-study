# Senior Lead Analyst case Study FAS Digital

## Forecasting NYC Monthly Pothole Complaints Using Prophet

### Business Context

New York City recieves thousands of street condition complaints every month.  Potholes in particular influence resdient satisfaction, operational costs for street maintenance, and broader perceptions of city service quality.  Effective forecasting helps the city:

- Allocate repair crews more efficiently
- Anticipate seasonal surges
- Improve service-level compliance
- Reduce safety risks and long repair queues

As a Senior LEad Data Analyst, your job is to transform open-source 311 data into actionable insights and a clear forecast narrative

### Your Task

Using the NYC 311 open dataset, build a monthly forecast of pothole compliants using Prophet (Meta's forecasting library) and produce a short insight-driven presentation.  We are evaluating analytical reasoning and executive story telling.  Send presentation and code to recruiter when complete.  Please spend 2-3 hours, indicate any gaps you would fill later given time.

## Additional Guidance

### Data
311 Service Requests -- https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Social-Services/311-Service-Requests/erm2-nwe9

You may filter for complaint type: "Street Condition" and descriptor: "Pothole". This can change, so please double check.

1. Executive Summary (1 slide)

   - 1 paragraph (3-5 sentences) at the top
   - Should frame the business context, importance, and high level results

   - Keep it concise, narrative, and leadership-ready

2. Descriptive Stats + Time-Series Framing (1 slide)
   - 1-2 annoted visuals only

3. Forecast Insights (Up to 3 slides - 1 big insight per slide)
   - Title must state the insight directly

4. Appendix (2-3 slides) (Optional)

Executive Briefing · Street Maintenance Strategy

NYC Pothole Complaints Forecast

What this report is: A 9-month predictive forecast of monthly NYC 311 pothole complaints (Aug 2026 – Apr 2027) modeled from 200 months of historical data (991,071 complaints).

Goal: To forecast the number of pothole complaints so that NYC Department of Transportation (DOT) can plan ahead to meet the demand. With significantly increased snowfall in 2026, pothole occurances will increase this year and DOT must be prepared to meet the moment.

Operational Advisory for Leadership

Due to unusually heavy snowfall in NYC this year, actual complaint volumes over the next ~3–4 months (Aug–Nov 2026) are likely to track higher than the baseline model prediction. Operations and maintenance crews should plan capacity toward the upper forecast range or the Event-Aware alternate model.

Model Selection & Empirical Validation

28 Prophet configurations evaluated. Multiplicative seasonality with linear growth won with 11.4% validation MAPE.

What is MAPE? Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE) measures average forecast error as a percentage of actual complaints: avg(|Actual − Predicted| / Actual) × 100%. Lower is better (11.4% error = 88.6% forecast accuracy).

Slide 7 · Methodology

Methodology, Parameters & Pipeline

Production Prophet pipeline: linear trend, multiplicative yearly seasonality, Bayesian uncertainty.

1. Data Preparation

  • Source: NYC 311 Service Requests Open Data.
  • Filter: Street Condition / Pothole.
  • Aggregation: 200 clean months (Dec 2009 – Jul 2026).
  • Cleaning: Dropped partial Aug 2026.

2. Prophet Model

  • Model Formula: y(t) = Trend × (1 + Yearly Seasonality).
  • Changepoint Prior: 0.01 (conservative).
  • Seasonality Prior: 100.0 (strong).
  • Uncertainty: 300 MCMC samples (95% CI).

3. Validation Framework

  • Window 1 (2023-24): 6.0% validation MAPE.
  • Window 2 (2024-25): 16.8% validation MAPE.
  • Cross-Validation: 14 rolling folds (21.7% MAPE).
  • Delivery: Pure static SVG in browser.
